Another way to launch

Monthly Digital Build Plan

For larger builds that benefit from a lower initial commitment and ongoing support. Tailored around the project, support level and agreed term.

What can a monthly plan include?

The agreed website or app build, maintenance, technical updates, support and selected content changes or improvements.

Each plan is quoted separately. It does not include unlimited development. Inclusions, responsibilities and the delivery term are confirmed before work begins. A follow-up qualification questionnaire helps establish scope and content readiness.

What does teodor dev tech build?

Business websites, WordPress websites, website redesigns, lead-focused pages, web apps and Android apps. The platform and scope depend on what suits your project.

Who do you work with?

Service businesses, growing brands and companies in South Africa and the U.S. that need stronger online presentation or practical digital tools.

How much will my project cost?

Projects are quoted around scope, page count, content readiness, features and support. Essential, Professional and Executive provide a starting point; a final quote is agreed before work begins.

Can I spread the cost of a larger build?

A monthly digital build arrangement may be suitable for a more involved website or app. The amount, term, support and inclusions are tailored to the project.

What happens after I enquire?

Your goals, timeline and requirements are reviewed to recommend a suitable package or tailored scope. Monthly-plan enquiries may include a follow-up qualification questionnaire before quoting.

Is the concept maker a finished website?

No. It creates a starting direction from curated themes and content patterns. It is an idea to discuss, with the design, content and working functionality built properly as part of your project.
